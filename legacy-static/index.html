01 · about

Curious about the why before the how

I'm an AI/ML engineer and a Master of Computer Applications graduate from Aligarh Muslim University, with an undergraduate foundation in Mathematics. I build at the intersection of machine learning, generative AI and software — from training models in PyTorch and TensorFlow to designing autonomous agents with Salesforce Agentforce and shipping Android apps in Kotlin.

Most recently I interned as a Salesforce & AI Developer at Infoglen, integrating Agentforce and Einstein capabilities into enterprise workflows. Before that, I led the AI/ML track at the Computer Science Society, mentoring peers through data preprocessing, model development and evaluation, and teaching school students prompt engineering and real-world AI.

Linux is my home environment for scripting and automation, and MATLAB has sharpened my numerical analysis. I'm currently exploring RLHF, ethical AI and agentic systems — and I'm looking for a team where I can turn that curiosity into impact.
